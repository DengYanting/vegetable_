Vegetables

Carrots:Crisp and naturally sweet, carrots are a versatile root vegetable and a source of beta-carotene, which the body converts into vitamin A.

Corn:Sweet, golden, and full of texture, corn is a versatile grain that can be enjoyed on the cob or added to a wide range of dishes.

Bell peppers:Crisp and mildly sweet, bell peppers bring bright color and a refreshing crunch to meals. They’re also a source of vitamin C and antioxidants.
